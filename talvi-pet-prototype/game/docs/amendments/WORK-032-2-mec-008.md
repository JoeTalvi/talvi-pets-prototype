## §2.7 View timing of the box — append

- Heart moment hold: while the Heart moment is playing (from `heart.earned` in an interior until 2200 ms or a skip after 600 ms), the dialogue view holds any box show and performs it at the moment's end. Combined with the fulfil hold above, the vignette box shows at max(fulfil hold release, moment end). The sim's dialogue state is untouched; a script advanced through the store during the moment shows its current line at the end; a script closed through the store cancels the moment; `state.reset` cancels it with the box hidden; `scene.changed` cancels it and shows a held box at once.
