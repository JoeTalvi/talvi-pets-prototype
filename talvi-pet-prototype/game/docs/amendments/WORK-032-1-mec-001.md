## R3 (walk, slide)

A point is walkable iff it is inside the scene's `walkArea` and outside every blocker: `walkable(p, walkArea, blockers)` in `src/sim/geometry.ts`.

- In the street, the blockers are `street.houses[].blockers`.
- In rooms there are none.

`slide()` in `src/sim/rules/avatar.ts` tries the full delta, then x only, then y only, each against `walkable`. If none is walkable it stays put. So pushing into a house's side slides along the blocker edge, and walking straight up into a house stops below its base.

There is no pathfinding (DEC-012), and houses never move (DEC-020/DEC-044).

## R4 (doors), street door placement

Each street door rect is placed this way:

- Its centre x is on the painted door, measured from the stage sprite's alpha and colour:
  - Starter House: `houses/starter-house-complete`.
  - Pet House: `houses/pet-house-lv6`, the stage at which the door opens under DEC-026.
- Its top edge is at the house anchor y (the sprite base and depth line), so the avatar standing in it draws in front of the house.
- It lies on walkable ground, below the house's blocker. The avatar enters by walking up into it from the street.

Door spawns sit just below their rect, walkable and outside every door rect.
