## §2.3 removal rows: the strip record (DEC-043, OQ-26)

- Data: per pet `heartCharms` (distinct types credited at any fulfilment: the types placed in the home room whose tags meet one of the fulfilled want's tag conditions, hidden included) and `strippedPending` (FIFO, distinct).
- When a Heart charm leaves its pet's room (remove, or replace by a different type) and no other slot of that room still holds it: `charm.stripped { roomId, slotId, typeId, petId }` directly after `charm.removed` / `charm.placed`, and the type is appended to `strippedPending` if absent. Placing it back into that room removes it from `strippedPending` (no event).
- A charm that earned no Heart never strips. Hearts, `fulfilledWantIds` and verbs never change.
- The reaction itself (one sad emote and one line the next time the pet looks for that charm), the `stripped` `Reaction` value and `lines.stripped` are **WORK-033-2**; this lane only records and queues.
