## R7 / R8 the chooser with counts (DEC-043, gate fork default 6)

- The sheet lists `chooserTypes(slotId)` in first-grant order, zero-count types included and **not moved**.
- Every card with copies in hand shows a "×n" badge (`tpp.chooser.copies`, n = `copiesOf(type)`) and carries `data-action="place"`; a click dispatches `placeCharm { slotId, typeId }` and closes the sheet (`ui.chooserClosed {pick}`).
- A card at zero copies is greyed (class `none-left`), shows "none left" (`tpp.chooser.noneLeft`) in place of the badge, has `aria-disabled="true"` and no `data-action`: a click dispatches nothing and the sheet stays open (the input layer's `input.tap` is the only log record).
- The header `tpp.chooser.count` ("{count} charms you own") counts the **copies in hand** among the listed cards (zero cards add 0).
- While open, the sheet re-renders on `items.granted`, `charm.placed` and `charm.removed` (badges, zero states and header update without reopening; the card strip keeps its scroll position).
- `tpp.chooser.empty` still shows only when no granted type has the slot's size class.
- Markup: one shared `charmCard()` in `src/ui/panels/templates/chooser.ts` renders every card for the game and for `/tools/panels.html`.
