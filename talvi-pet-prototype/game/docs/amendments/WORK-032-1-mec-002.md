## Feed clock (tick step 3)

The feed banks no time and fires no `roundup.fired` while any dialogue is open (`dialogue.current !== null`). This covers orientation, intro, want announcement, vignette, phase message and completion message alike.

It also banks nothing while the orientation is still owed (`!session.orientationShown`). So on a fresh save no round-up can fire, and no time is banked, until the opening conversation has closed and the player can walk.

Time banked before a dialogue opens is kept. The clock resumes from it after the last `advanceDialogue` closes the box. For example, with 100 ms banked and an interval of 250 ms, the first round-up fires 150 ms after the close.
