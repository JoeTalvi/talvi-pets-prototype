## Stars per space (R1–R4)

- `starsOfSpace(content, state, ref)` is the one formula for rooms and sites (distinct type `starsFirstCopy`, repeats `starsRepeat`; max = `starsFirstCopy × slots`); `starsOf(content, state, roomId)` is its room wrapper (signature kept).
- A site's Stars are derived, never stored (`spaces[siteId]` has no `stars`; fork 2). Garden max = 4 × 6 = 24; `arrivalAtStars` 8 = two distinct charms.
- After a site placement the chain emits `space.stars.changed { siteId, from, to, delta, max }`; rooms keep `stars.changed { roomId, … }`. A site change never moves a room's Stars.
